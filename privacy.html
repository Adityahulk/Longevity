Privacy policy

This policy explains how Antiaging Labs Pvt. Ltd. processes information when you use Aevum, including when you choose to connect Oura or add health information to your account.

Aevum is an early-stage personal health and longevity information product. Wearable and genetic data sources are optional.

Effective 2 October 2026 · Antiaging Labs Pvt. Ltd. · Hyderabad, India

Information you choose to add

The information Aevum processes depends on the features and sources you use. It may include:

  • Account information: name, email, login and preferences.
  • Profile and lifestyle information: goals and answers you choose to provide, including exercise, sleep, diet and relevant health context.
  • Health information: laboratory measurements or reports, optional genetic files or derived findings, and wearable measurements or summaries you connect or import.
  • Product interactions: questions you send to Ask Aevum, consent choices, imports and actions you take in the product.
  • Technical information: information needed to secure, operate and troubleshoot the service.

Connecting Oura

If you choose Connect Oura, you leave Aevum and sign in directly with Oura. Oura asks you to authorize the requested read-only scopes. Aevum does not receive your Oura password. You can decline or disconnect the connection at any time.

After authorization, Aevum’s self-hosted Open Wearables connection service retrieves the permitted data from Oura, syncs and normalizes it, and passes supported measurements to your Aevum account. Depending on your permissions and data availability, this may include daily sleep, activity and readiness summaries, heart-rate information and workouts. Not every metric is available for every account or used by Aevum.

Oura processes information under its own terms and privacy information. Connecting Oura does not give Aevum permission to write to or change your Oura account.

How Aevum uses information

Aevum uses information to provide account features, organize measurements, show personal patterns and evidence, support recommendations and experiments, answer questions you ask, troubleshoot the service and protect accounts. We do not sell personal health information or use it for targeted advertising.

When information is shared with service providers

  • Open Wearables: processes connected wearable data and provider authorization to operate the connection and sync service. Aevum operates this self-hosted service.
  • OpenAI API: when you use Ask Aevum, your question and relevant personal context selected to answer it may be sent to OpenAI for response generation. Ask is optional. Uploaded source documents and raw genotype files are not sent to the language model; structured, relevant findings may be included in the selected context.
  • Railway: hosts Aevum application services and databases used to operate the product.
  • Clinician review: if you request a clinician conversation, authorized Aevum staff may access your report to arrange it and share relevant information with the clinician for that requested review.

We may also disclose information if required by law or needed to protect the service and its users. Service providers process information to provide their services to Aevum; they are not authorized by Aevum to use it for advertising.

Storage and deletion

Aevum retains account and health information while needed to provide the service and maintain your account. The application encrypts stored health records. Wearable access credentials are managed by Open Wearables and are not exposed in the Aevum browser interface. When an account is deleted, information is removed from active application storage; copies may remain in routine backups until those backups are rotated. We may retain limited records where required by law or needed to protect the service.

Your controls

  • Use Aevum account settings to export or delete your account and associated records where those features are available.
  • Disconnect a wearable in Aevum or revoke access through your provider account. Disconnecting stops future syncs; previously imported records remain unless you also delete them.
  • Contact us to ask about access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests. We may need to verify your identity before acting.

These controls do not delete information held independently by Oura or another provider. You can manage that information with the provider directly.

Health information and security

Aevum provides informational wellness and longevity support. It does not diagnose disease, prescribe treatment or replace a qualified clinician. Measurements may be incomplete, delayed, device-dependent or reported by you. Interpretations can be uncertain and should be discussed with a healthcare professional when they affect a health decision.

We use technical and organizational measures to protect information, but no internet service can promise absolute security. Please use a unique password and contact us if you suspect unauthorized access.
