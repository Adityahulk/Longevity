Connect a wearable.

Connect a supported provider from your Aevum account. You’ll choose the provider and authorize access there. Wearable data is optional; you can use Aevum with the information you already have.

Continue in Aevum

Sign in, open You, then choose Wearables. The providers available to connect are shown in your account. Depending on your device, you may also be able to import a health-data export.
