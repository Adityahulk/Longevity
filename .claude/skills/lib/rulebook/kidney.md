# Rulebook — Kidney

## R-KID-01 — Mildly reduced eGFR
- **trigger**: `egfr 60–89 mL/min/1.73m²`
- **tier**: 2
- **root_cause_hypothesis**: aging, hypertension, NSAID overuse, diabetes, dehydration.
- **N**: moderate protein (1.0–1.2 g/kg, not >1.6) · 2.5–3L water/day · sodium <2.3g/day.
- **T**: blood-pressure-focused — Zone 2 + resistance.
- **S**: discontinue daily NSAIDs (ibuprofen, diclofenac) — common occult driver.
- **retest_signal**: eGFR ↑ 5–10 mL/min if NSAID-driven.
- **evidence_tier**: E1

## R-KID-02 — Significantly reduced eGFR — **TIER 1**
- **trigger**: `egfr < 60 mL/min/1.73m²`
- **tier**: 1
- Nephrology referral. Cap protein at 0.8g/kg until cleared. No creatine, no high-dose Vit D, no NSAID, no contrast imaging without nephrologist sign-off.
- **evidence_tier**: E1

## R-KID-03 — Mild creatinine elevation
- **trigger**: `creatinine 1.2–1.4` (M) OR `1.1–1.3` (F) AND `egfr ≥ 60`
- **tier**: 3
- Often muscle-mass driven in lifters (not kidney). Use cystatin C as confirmation if available. Hydration audit; re-test in 4 weeks fasted with no creatine for 5+ days.
- **evidence_tier**: E2

## R-KID-04 — Electrolyte derangement — **TIER 1**
- **trigger**: `potassium < 3.0 OR > 5.5` OR `sodium < 130 OR > 150`
- **tier**: 1
- Emergent — physician contact same-day. Often medication-driven (diuretics, ACE inhibitors).
- **evidence_tier**: E1
