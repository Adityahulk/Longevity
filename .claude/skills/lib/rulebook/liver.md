# Rulebook — Liver

## R-LIV-01 — Elevated ALT or GGT
- **trigger**: `alt > 30` (M) OR `> 25` (F) OR `ggt > 30` (M) OR `> 20` (F)
- **tier**: 2
- **root_cause_hypothesis**: NAFLD (most common driver in Indians, even at normal weight), alcohol, medications, viral, autoimmune.
- **N**: drop alcohol entirely for 90 days · drop fructose (no juice, no HFCS) · drop ultra-processed food · 30g protein/meal · TRE 14:10.
- **T**: Zone 2 + resistance — most evidence-based NAFLD intervention.
- **S**: consider milk thistle (silymarin 200mg × 3/day) if intake confirms heavy past alcohol [E3].
- **retest_signal**: ALT ↓ 30–50% in 90 days if NAFLD-driven.
- **evidence_tier**: E1

## R-LIV-02 — Severely elevated transaminases — **TIER 1**
- **trigger**: `alt ≥ 3× ULN` OR `ast ≥ 3× ULN` (i.e., >120 U/L typical)
- **tier**: 1
- Hepatology referral. Pause supplement recommendations until cleared.
- **evidence_tier**: E1

## R-LIV-03 — Low albumin
- **trigger**: `albumin < 3.8 g/dL`
- **tier**: 2
- **root_cause_hypothesis**: low protein intake, chronic inflammation, kidney loss.
- **N**: protein audit — target 1.2–1.6g/kg body weight from whole foods.
- **S**: whey isolate post-workout if dietary protein insufficient.
- **retest_signal**: ↑ 0.2–0.4 g/dL in 90 days.
- **evidence_tier**: E1

## R-LIV-04 — Elevated FIB-4
- **trigger**: `fib_4 > 1.30` (Tier 3) OR `> 2.67` (Tier 2 advanced-fibrosis screen)
- **tier**: 3 if 1.30–2.67; 2 if >2.67
- Non-invasive liver fibrosis screen. Combine R-LIV-01 protocols. If >2.67, hepatology referral for FibroScan or elastography.
- **evidence_tier**: E2

## R-LIV-05 — AST/ALT ratio pattern
- **trigger**: `ast/alt > 2` AND elevated transaminases
- **tier**: 2
- Classic alcohol pattern. Address alcohol intake explicitly in protocol.
- **evidence_tier**: E1
