# Rulebook — Thyroid

## R-THY-01 — Subclinical hypothyroidism
- **trigger**: `tsh 4.0–10 μIU/mL` AND `free_t4` normal
- **tier**: 2
- **root_cause_hypothesis**: autoimmune (Hashimoto's — check anti-TPO), iodine deficiency/excess, selenium deficiency, stress.
- **N**: iodine from sea fish 2×/wk or 1/4 tsp iodized salt/day · Brazil nuts 2/day (selenium).
- **S**: Vitamin D to >40 ng/mL · selenium 100–200mcg/day for 90 days.
- **Recommend**: endocrinology consult if TSH > 6 or anti-TPO positive; do not start levothyroxine without physician.
- **evidence_tier**: E1

## R-THY-02 — Overt hypothyroidism — **TIER 1**
- **trigger**: `tsh > 10` OR (`tsh > 4` AND low Free T4)
- **tier**: 1
- Endocrinology referral.
- **evidence_tier**: E1

## R-THY-03 — Hyperthyroidism — **TIER 1**
- **trigger**: `tsh < 0.1` AND high Free T4 / Free T3
- **tier**: 1
- Endocrinology referral. Rule out Graves', toxic nodule, thyroiditis.
- **evidence_tier**: E1

## R-THY-04 — Positive anti-TPO
- **trigger**: `anti_tpo > 34 IU/mL` (regardless of TSH)
- **tier**: 2
- Autoimmune thyroid signal (Hashimoto's). Monitor TSH q6 months. Address Vit D, selenium, gluten-sensitivity if symptomatic.
- **evidence_tier**: E1

## R-THY-05 — Low Free T3 (with normal TSH)
- **trigger**: `free_t3 < 2.5 pg/mL` AND `tsh` normal
- **tier**: 3
- Possible non-thyroidal-illness pattern (chronic stress, low caloric intake, over-training, severe nutrient deficit). Address upstream drivers; do not supplement T3.
- **evidence_tier**: E2
