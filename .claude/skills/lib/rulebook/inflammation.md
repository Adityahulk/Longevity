# Rulebook — Inflammation

## R-INF-01 — Elevated hsCRP (chronic-inflammation range)
- **trigger**: `hs_crp 1.0–10 mg/L` AND no acute illness
- **tier**: 2
- **root_cause_hypothesis**: ultra-processed food, seed-oil excess, sleep debt, chronic stress, visceral fat, gut dysbiosis, oral health (gingivitis/periodontitis — often occult), low omega-3:6 ratio.
- **N**: anti-inflammatory diet — turmeric 1 tsp/day with black pepper, ginger, berries, leafy greens, fatty fish · drop ultra-processed (chips, biscuits, namkeen, packaged sweets, refined oils — palm, rice bran, sunflower).
- **T**: Zone 2 (resolves IL-6 via myokine pathway).
- **R**: 8h sleep non-negotiable.
- **S**: omega-3 2g EPA+DHA/day [E1], curcumin 500mg × 2/day with food [E2]. Check dental — periodontitis is a common occult driver.
- **M**: stress reduction — cortisol drives inflammation.
- **retest_signal**: hsCRP ↓ to <1.0 mg/L typical in 90 days if cause is lifestyle.
- **evidence_tier**: E1

## R-INF-02 — Acute hsCRP — **TIER 1**
- **trigger**: `hs_crp > 10 mg/L`
- **tier**: 1
- Likely acute infection or flare. Repeat in 2 weeks. If persistently >10, medical workup (autoimmune, occult infection, malignancy).
- **evidence_tier**: E1

## R-INF-03 — Elevated homocysteine
- **trigger**: `homocysteine > 10 μmol/L`
- **tier**: 2 if 10–15; 1 if >15
- **root_cause_hypothesis**: B12 / folate / B6 deficiency, MTHFR polymorphism, kidney function, alcohol.
- **S**: B-complex with methylated forms (methylcobalamin 1000mcg, methylfolate 400mcg, P5P 25mg) daily for 12 weeks. [E1]
- **N**: leafy greens, eggs, fish.
- **retest_signal**: homocysteine ↓ 30–40% on methylated B-complex in 12 weeks.
- **evidence_tier**: E1

## R-INF-04 — Elevated NLR (neutrophil:lymphocyte ratio) — NEW
- **trigger**: `nlr > 3.0` (derived)
- **tier**: 2
- **root_cause_hypothesis**: systemic inflammation, acute or chronic stress, occult infection, accelerating aging signal.
- Combine R-INF-01 protocols. If isolated finding without other inflammation markers, repeat in 4–6 weeks before acting.
- **evidence_tier**: E3

## R-INF-05 — Elevated MHR (monocyte:HDL ratio) — NEW
- **trigger**: `mhr > 0.013` (derived)
- **tier**: 3
- **root_cause_hypothesis**: emerging cardiovascular-inflammation index; correlates with carotid plaque burden and CV events.
- Combine R-INF-01 + R-LIP-04 (raise HDL) protocols.
- **evidence_tier**: E3
