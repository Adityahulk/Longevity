# Rulebook — Metabolic

## R-MET-01 — Insulin resistance
- **trigger**: `homa_ir > 1.5` OR `insulin_fasting > 10` OR `tg_hdl_ratio > 2.5`
- **tier**: 2
- **root_cause_hypothesis**: refined-carb dominance, sedentary work, low muscle mass, sleep debt, chronic stress.
- **N**: 30–40g protein/meal · 3 meals · eating window 10–12h (start 14:10, progress to 16:8 after wk 4) · whole-grain carbs (oats, millets, whole-wheat roti, brown rice) · ≥30g fiber · drop fruit juice (whole fruit OK) · no added sugar.
- **T**: Zone 2 cardio 150–180 min/wk (4×45min at 60–70% maxHR or MAF=180−age) · 3× resistance/wk full-body compounds · 8–10k steps/day.
- **R**: 7.5–8h sleep · caffeine cutoff 10h before bed · alcohol ≤2 drinks/wk.
- **S**: Vit D if <30, Mg glycinate if intake low, omega-3 2g EPA+DHA if low fish intake. Berberine 500mg × 3/day **only after physician sign-off** if HbA1c 5.7–6.4. [E2]
- **M**: 10-min daily box-breathing (5-5-5) — cortisol mediates glucose · weekly nature walk.
- **retest_signal**: fasting insulin ↓ 30–50%, HOMA-IR < 1.0, HbA1c down 0.2–0.4%.
- **evidence_tier**: E1

## R-MET-02 — Prediabetes
- **trigger**: `hba1c 5.7–6.4` OR `glucose_fasting 100–125`
- **tier**: 2
- **root_cause_hypothesis**: glycation accumulating over past 90 days.
- All R-MET-01 plus:
- **N**: post-meal 10-min walk (mandatory) · 1 tbsp ACV in water before highest-carb meal · cinnamon 1 tsp/day (light evidence; safe).
- **T**: add 1× VO₂max session/wk (4×4-min intervals at 90% HR / "very hard").
- **S**: discuss berberine 500mg × 3/day or metformin with physician — physician decision.
- **retest_signal**: HbA1c down 0.3–0.5% in 90 days achievable.
- **evidence_tier**: E1

## R-MET-03 — Diabetes range — **TIER 1**
- **trigger**: `hba1c ≥ 6.5` OR `glucose_fasting ≥ 126`
- **tier**: 1
- Surface the **physician referral** callout. Endocrinology consult before any program.
- Tessera protocol can run in parallel but does not replace medical care.
- **evidence_tier**: E1

## R-MET-04 — Hyperuricemia
- **trigger**: `uric_acid > 6.5` (M) OR `> 5.5` (F)
- **tier**: 2
- **root_cause_hypothesis**: fructose, alcohol (especially beer), red meat, dehydration, genetic.
- **N**: drop fructose (no juice, no HFCS, limit mango/banana to 1/day) · drop beer · 3L water/day · tart cherry juice (light evidence).
- **S**: discuss with physician if symptomatic (gout history) or > 8 mg/dL.
- **retest_signal**: uric acid ↓ 1–2 mg/dL in 60 days with dietary changes alone.
- **evidence_tier**: E2
