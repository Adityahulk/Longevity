# Tessera Phenotype Atlas

Phenotypes are *labels*, not destinies. A phenotype captures the dominant pattern a client presents with, and shapes the **emphasis** of the 5-domain protocol — what to lead with, what to deprioritize, how to talk about goals.

A client can fit one *primary* phenotype + one or two *overlays* (e.g. "Lean-metabolically-excellent + stress overlay + atopic overlay").

---

## P-01 — Lean Metabolically Excellent

- **Diagnostic pattern**: BMI 18–24, waist <85cm (M) / <75cm (F), HOMA-IR <1.2, HbA1c <5.4, lipids in optimal, ApoB <80, insulin <7, ALT/AST optimal, no metabolic syndrome features.
- **Common in**: young (20–35) urban Indian adults with active jobs / sports backgrounds.
- **Protocol emphasis**: this is an **optimization** phenotype, not a treatment one. Lead with healthspan-extension framing (Zone 2, resistance, sleep, nutrient sufficiency). Don't preach about IR they don't have. Focus on the *other* layers (stress, sleep, micronutrients, atopic, hormonal optimization within range).
- **Voice**: confident, peer-to-peer, future-focused ("you're already in great metabolic shape; the upgrade is on stress, sleep, and nutrient optimization").

## P-02 — Lean Metabolically Unhealthy ("Thin-fat" / TOFI)

- **Diagnostic pattern**: BMI <25 but waist >90cm (M) / >80cm (F), HOMA-IR >1.5, TG:HDL >2.5, AIP elevated, fatty liver signal (ALT, GGT mildly up), ApoB borderline-high — *despite normal BMI*.
- **Common in**: 30–50yo Indian adults, sedentary professional, normal weight by Western metric but visceral adiposity.
- **Protocol emphasis**: aggressive metabolic intervention. Body composition shift over scale-weight. Resistance training is non-negotiable. Caloric balance with high-protein anchor. Eating window. AIP and TG:HDL are the key tracking markers, not just LDL.
- **Voice**: re-frame the mirror — "your blood is telling a different story than your weight."

## P-03 — Overweight / Obese (frank metabolic syndrome)

- **Diagnostic pattern**: BMI >25, multiple metabolic-syndrome features, HbA1c >5.6, HOMA-IR >2, lipids dysregulated, ALT/GGT elevated.
- **Protocol emphasis**: caloric deficit + protein anchor + Zone 2 dose ramp. May warrant medical co-management (GLP-1, metformin). Long arc — 6–12 months, not 90 days.
- **Voice**: hopeful, sustainable, no shame; reframe to "your biology is responsive; here's the order of operations."

## P-04 — Stress / Cortisol-Dominant

- **Diagnostic pattern**: cortisol AM near top of range or elevated, HRV depressed, sleep <7h, PHQ-2/GAD-2 elevated or self-reported stress ≥7. Often with: secondary testosterone suppression (M), menstrual irregularity (F), atopic flares, mild lipid drift.
- **Common in**: high-achievers, entrepreneurs, shift workers, new parents, caregivers.
- **Protocol emphasis**: Recovery + Mind domains lead. Sleep first (anchored wake, screen curfew, environment). Daily breath + meditation. Phone curfew. Often: ashwagandha. Often: reduce caffeine. Training: dial down volume, prioritize Zone 2 over HIIT during recovery phase. Nutrition: stable meals, don't add fasting stress on top.
- **Voice**: validating, calm, "you're not weak — your nervous system has been running hot."

## P-05 — Inflammatory / Burnt-Out

- **Diagnostic pattern**: hsCRP >1.0, homocysteine >10, low Vit D, often with co-morbidity (NAFLD, IR, atopic, autoimmune tendency, periodontal disease).
- **Protocol emphasis**: address upstream drivers (sleep, diet, dental, gut, Vit D). Anti-inflammatory eating pattern. Omega-3. Curcumin. Often dental cleaning is the highest-leverage step. Methylated B-complex if homocysteine elevated.
- **Voice**: forensic — "let's find what's driving the smoke."

## P-06 — Atopic / Allergic Overlay

- **Diagnostic pattern**: eosinophils >6% (often >8%), often IgE elevated, HPI positive for rhinitis / asthma / eczema / food allergy.
- Usually paired with a primary phenotype (e.g., "P-01 + atopic overlay").
- **Protocol emphasis**: Vit D >40 ng/mL, omega-3, quercetin, gut barrier (fermented foods + fiber), HEPA filter, allergen avoidance, identify triggers via elimination trial. Refer to allergist if symptomatic.
- **Voice**: detective — "let's find your triggers."

## P-07 — Hormone-Suppressed (M low-T workup or F low-E2)

- **Diagnostic pattern (M)**: testosterone_total <400, often with: visceral fat, low Vit D, sleep <7h, low resistance training. Or, in lean men, often M-17 (lifestyle-driven via stress + sleep + Vit D).
- **Diagnostic pattern (F)**: low estradiol in pre-menopause, hypothalamic amenorrhea, RED-S signal.
- **Protocol emphasis (M)**: address upstream first (sleep, Vit D, stress, resistance training, body composition). Direct T optimization only after foundations. Endocrinology referral if T <250.
- **Protocol emphasis (F)**: caloric availability, body fat ≥18%, OB-GYN referral.
- **Voice**: address foundations before reaching for direct hormone interventions.

## P-08 — Optimizer / Near-Optimal

- **Diagnostic pattern**: all markers in or near optimal ranges, no Tier 1 findings, intake shows already-strong baseline (sleep ≥7.5h, training 5+/wk, Vit D >40, low stress).
- **Common in**: motivated 30–55yo who've been working at their health for years.
- **Protocol emphasis**: marginal gains. Research-grade markers (GlycA, omega-3 index, ApoB <60, T peak optimization, full hormone panel). Advanced interventions (Zone-2 ramps to VO2max work, micronutrient precision dosing, methylation-aware B-complex if MTHFR known, sleep optimization with wearable feedback).
- **Voice**: peer respect — "you've earned the right to optimize."

## How to classify

`tessera-rootcause-phenotype` classifies by:

1. Counting **primary diagnostic features** met (e.g., BMI band, HOMA-IR, waist, lipid pattern).
2. Checking **intake signals** for overlays (stress, atopic, sleep debt).
3. Falling through priority: if metabolic syndrome features, P-03 dominates; else check P-02 thin-fat criteria; else P-01 or P-08 depending on rule-fires count.
4. Adding overlay phenotypes (P-04, P-05, P-06) on top of the primary.

The phenotype is written on the **cover** of the report, alongside the bio-age. It tells the client where they are and frames everything downstream.

## Phenotype-specific protocol-author hints

Each phenotype shifts the *emphasis* of the 5-domain protocol:

| Phenotype | Lead domain | Deprioritize | Voice |
|---|---|---|---|
| P-01 Lean-Met-Excellent | Mind, Supplements (precision) | aggressive caloric/lipid prescriptions | peer, future-focused |
| P-02 Thin-fat | Nutrition, Training | scale-weight obsession | reframe the mirror |
| P-03 Overweight metabolic | Nutrition (caloric), Training | complex micronutrient layering | hopeful, sustainable, long arc |
| P-04 Stress-dominant | Recovery, Mind | high-volume training, fasting | validating |
| P-05 Inflammatory | Nutrition (anti-inf), Medical (dental) | every supplement before finding the driver | forensic |
| P-06 Atopic overlay | Supplements, Recovery (env), Medical | aggressive interventions before identifying triggers | detective |
| P-07 Hormone-suppressed | Recovery, Training, Nutrition | direct hormone supplementation as first move | foundations-first |
| P-08 Optimizer | Supplements (precision), advanced retesting | back-to-basics if already nailed | peer respect, marginal gains |

## Notes

- Phenotypes are not labels of identity — they're snapshots of biology + lifestyle right now. Re-classify at every retest.
- Multiple overlays are common (e.g., P-01 + P-04 + P-06 for a stressed, lean, atopic 25-year-old like Aditya).
- Phenotype + root-cause graph + safety screen together produce the protocol shape. Phenotype gives the *voice* and *emphasis*; rootcause gives the *attack points*; safety constrains the *what's allowed*.
