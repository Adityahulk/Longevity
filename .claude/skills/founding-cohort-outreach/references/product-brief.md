# Product Brief — Antiaging Labs

Read this before writing any outreach or content. It is the source of truth for what the product
is, who it's for, and — critically — what you are and are not allowed to claim. The whole brand
runs on being more honest than the rest of the longevity market; outreach that overclaims breaks
that on contact.

## One-liner

Antiaging Labs is **the integration layer for your biology**: it pulls genome, bloodwork, and
wearable data into one live "Biological Twin" and an engine that estimates your biological age
and compiles a daily protocol to slow how fast you're aging. Built and calibrated for South Asian
biology. Site: **antiaging-labs.com**.

**Naming it in a message — keep the genome in the frame.** The differentiator is the multi-omic
*integration*, so don't reduce it to "bloodwork + a wearable" (that's what makes it sound like
every other tracker). Default short-form description: "pulls your DNA, bloodwork and wearable into
one read of how fast you're aging." DEXA/MRI come up on the call or for the imaging tier, not in a
cold opener. Genome + labs + wearable is the minimum that conveys what the product actually is.

## Who it's for (ICP)

Primary for the first cohort: **Indian metro professionals, ~28-50, high-earning and
data-literate.** Founders, tech and finance operators, doctors, and serious athletes in
Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Pune.

Strong signals someone is a fit:
- Already wears an Oura / Whoop / Apple Watch and actually looks at the data.
- Has had bloodwork and been told "everything's normal" but doesn't feel optimized.
- Optimizes other parts of life by the numbers (training PRs, business metrics).
- Curious about biological age, healthspan, VO2 max, ApoB, longevity science (Attia/Huberman-adjacent).
- Can comfortably spend ₹28K-65K on their health and wants rigor, not a wellness vibe.

Not a fit (and worth disqualifying early, to protect consult quality):
- Wants fast weight loss or aesthetics in 30 days.
- Wants supplements with zero behaviour change.
- Has an active condition needing primary clinical care (we work alongside doctors, not instead).
- Won't do bloodwork or share wearable data.

## The wedge: built for South Asian biology

This is the sharpest differentiator for the Indian audience and should anchor most messaging.
Most labs and longevity tools read your results against Western reference ranges. South Asians
have meaningfully different baselines (e.g. higher visceral fat and cardiometabolic risk at lower
BMI, different lipid and diabetes risk patterns). "Your numbers are normal" is often measured
against the wrong population. Antiaging Labs reads markers against South Asian reference ranges
and calibrates protocols accordingly. For an Indian professional who's been told they're fine,
this reframes the whole thing — and it's true, which is why it works.

## What's genuinely true — safe to say

Use these freely; they're on the site and defensible:
- One health record that unifies bloodwork, genome, wearables, and imaging into a single engine.
- Biological age via **validated clocks**: Levine PhenoAge (2018) and Klemera-Doubal (KDM-BA),
  computed as transparent benchmarks and tracked before/after each cycle.
- A **50+ / 87-marker** panel read against **South Asian reference ranges**.
- A **traceable, rule-based report** — tap any finding (e.g. elevated ApoB) and see the exact
  rule and data that triggered it. No black box.
- A **4-lever daily protocol**: nutrition, training, recovery, supplements/co-factors.
- **Physician-in-the-loop**: every protocol passes a clinical safety filter (aligned to KDIGO /
  2021 CKD-EPI) and a medical advisor before it reaches the user. We work alongside your doctor.
- **Live Biological Twin** (Deep tier) that updates from Oura/Whoop/Apple Watch telemetry.
- Models informed by large population cohorts (UK Biobank, NHANES) — non-linear, not one formula.
- A **research network** of working scientists/clinicians (e.g. Carlos Trujillo, clinical
  geneticist) who review methodology.
- A measure → intervene → **re-test** loop: the 90-day retest shows the actual marker delta.
- A public **engineering whitepaper** and a **sample report** exist to show, not just tell.

## Off-limits — do not claim

These break the brand's honesty and can mislead on health. Never write them, even implied, even
to win a consult:
- Any guarantee or promise to "reverse aging," extend lifespan, or cure/treat disease.
- Specific outcome numbers we haven't measured ("drop 10 years in 90 days", "everyone lowers ApoB
  30%"). No invented stats, no invented testimonials or user counts.
- "Diagnosis" or "treatment" framing — this is health-optimization guidance, not medical care,
  and does not replace a physician.
- FDA/clinical-approval implications, or that scans/genome are sold as routine (they're on
  indication/referral).
- Anything implying it's a magic pill or effortless. The program asks for real behaviour change;
  say so.

When a draft strays near these, pull it back to the measured version: we **measure** biological
age with validated clocks, **move markers**, and **re-test** to prove it.
