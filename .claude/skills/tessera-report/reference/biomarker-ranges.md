# Biomarker Reference Ranges — Optimal vs. Normal

For every marker, three ranges are listed:

- **Optimal** — where Tessera wants you (associated with the lowest all-cause mortality / best healthspan signal in long-term cohort data).
- **Acceptable** — within standard lab "normal," still inside the population reference interval.
- **Off-target** — outside acceptable; flagged as Tier 2 (single deviation) or Tier 1 (clinically significant) per the rulebook.

Sex-specific ranges marked **(M)** and **(F)**. Indian lab convention used throughout — convert to PhenoAge units only when feeding the formula.

## 2. Glucose & Insulin (metabolic)

| Marker | Unit | Optimal | Acceptable | Off-target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fasting Glucose | mg/dL | 75–88 | 70–99 | ≥100 (impaired); ≥126 → **Tier 1** (diabetes range) |
| Fasting Insulin | μIU/mL | 2–6 | 2–10 | >10 (early insulin resistance); >25 → **Tier 1** |
| HbA1c | % | <5.3 | 5.4–5.6 | 5.7–6.4 (prediabetes); ≥6.5 → **Tier 1** |
| HOMA-IR (derived) | — | <1.0 | 1.0–1.5 | >1.5 (insulin resistance); >2.5 → **Tier 1** |
| TG : HDL ratio (derived, mg/dL) | — | <1.5 | 1.5–2.5 | >2.5 (insulin resistance proxy in Indians) |

**Derived calculations:**
- HOMA-IR = (Fasting Glucose mg/dL × Fasting Insulin μIU/mL) / 405
- TG : HDL = Triglycerides ÷ HDL (both in mg/dL)

## Notes on Indian lab quirks

- **WBC units**: Thyrocare reports as `×10³/μL` or `K/μL`. Redcliffe sometimes reports `cells/μL` — divide by 1000.
- **HbA1c** comes with eAG (estimated average glucose) — eAG is informational, use HbA1c % as the primary value.
- **eGFR** is not always reported — derive it via CKD-EPI 2021 if creatinine, age, sex are present.
- **Lp(a)** is often reported in `nmol/L` by SRL and Thyrocare new panels — divide by 2.5 to approximate mg/dL.
- **Fasting** status is often not enforced in walk-ins. If the lab notes "non-fasting," glucose and triglycerides should be interpreted cautiously and flagged.

## Tier assignment rule (use in the report)

For each marker:

1. Look at the value.
2. If it falls in "Optimal" → **Tier 4** (maintain).
3. If in "Acceptable" → **Tier 3** (secondary target, light nudge).
4. If "Off-target" *and* the marker has explicit Tier 1 thresholds above that are met → **Tier 1** (refer to doctor).
5. Otherwise off-target → **Tier 2** (primary intervention target).

A panel with mostly Tier 4 markers gets a celebratory framing; one with multiple Tier 2 markers gets a serious, action-oriented framing. Tier 1 always gets the medical-advisory callout first.
