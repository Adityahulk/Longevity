# Tessera Rulebook — Biomarker → Tier → 5-Domain Interventions

This is the operational rulebook the report uses to translate biomarkers + intake into a personalized protocol. Each rule has four parts:

1. **Trigger** — biomarker pattern (one or more values + optional intake conditions).
2. **Root cause hypothesis** — what's likely driving the pattern.
3. **Domain interventions** — recommendations across Nutrition, Training, Recovery/Sleep, Supplements, Mind/Wellness.
4. **Re-test signal** — what to expect to move at Day 90.

Rules stack: a person can fire 5–10 rules at once. When two rules disagree (e.g., one says "increase protein," another says "reduce protein"), the doctor-gated path applies — flag for medical review.

The 5 protocol domains, in order:

- **N** — Nutrition
- **T** — Training
- **R** — Recovery & Sleep
- **S** — Supplements
- **M** — Mind & Wellness

## METABOLIC

### R-MET-01 — Insulin resistance (HOMA-IR > 1.5 or fasting insulin > 10)
**Root cause**: refined-carb dominance, sedentary work, low muscle mass, sleep debt, chronic stress.
- **N**: 30–40g protein per meal · 3 meals · eating window 10–12h (start at 14:10, progress to 16:8 after week 4) · carbs from intact whole grains (rolled oats, millets, whole-wheat roti, brown rice — no maida/white bread/sugar) · target 30–40g fiber/day · drop fruit juice, switch fruit to whole · no added sugar.
- **T**: Zone 2 cardio 150–180 min/week (4 × 45min walks/cycles at 60–70% max HR or MAF HR = 180 − age) · resistance 3×/week full-body (compounds: squat, hinge, push, pull) · 8–10k steps/day baseline.
- **R**: 7.5–8h sleep · caffeine cutoff 10h before bed · alcohol drop to ≤2 drinks/week.
- **S**: only if biomarkers indicate (Vitamin D if <30, Mg if intake low, omega-3 2g EPA+DHA if low fish intake). Berberine 500mg × 3/day **only after physician sign-off** if HbA1c 5.7–6.4.
- **M**: 10-min daily breathing (5-5-5 box) — cortisol mediates glucose; weekly nature walk.
- **Re-test signal**: fasting insulin ↓ 30–50%, HOMA-IR < 1.0, HbA1c down 0.2–0.4%.

### R-MET-02 — Prediabetes (HbA1c 5.7–6.4)
**Root cause**: glycation accumulating over the past 90 days.
- All of R-MET-01 +
- **N**: post-meal 10-min walk (mandatory) · vinegar-before-meals (1 tbsp ACV in water before highest-carb meal) · cinnamon 1 tsp/day (light evidence; safe).
- **T**: add 1× VO₂max session/week (4×4-min intervals at 90% HR or "very hard").
- **S**: discuss berberine 500mg × 3/day or metformin with physician — physician decision.
- **Re-test signal**: HbA1c down 0.3–0.5% in 90 days achievable.

### R-MET-03 — Diabetes range (HbA1c ≥ 6.5 or fasting glucose ≥ 126) — **TIER 1**
- Report-level: must surface the **physician referral** callout. Recommend endocrinology consult before any program.
- Tessera protocol can still proceed in parallel but does not replace medical care.

### R-MET-04 — Hyperuricemia (Uric acid > 6.5 M / > 5.5 F)
**Root cause**: fructose, alcohol (especially beer), red meat, dehydration, genetic.
- **N**: drop fructose (no fruit juice, no HFCS, limit mango/banana to 1/day) · drop beer · 3L water/day · cherries / tart cherry juice (light evidence).
- **S**: discuss with physician if symptomatic (gout history) or > 8 mg/dL.
- **Re-test signal**: uric acid ↓ 1–2 mg/dL in 60 days with dietary changes alone.

## LIPIDS / CARDIOVASCULAR

### R-LIP-01 — High ApoB / LDL (ApoB > 100 or LDL > 130)
**Root cause**: saturated fat intake, refined-carb load, low fiber, genetic.
- **N**: shift saturated fat → mono/poly (mustard, sesame, peanut, olive oils replace ghee/butter for most cooking; ghee/butter to 1–2 tsp/day) · 30g fiber, of which 10g soluble (oats, psyllium, beans, apples) · 30g almonds/walnuts/day · drop refined carbs.
- **T**: Zone 2 5×/week 45 min — directly lowers ApoB.
- **S**: psyllium 5–10g pre-meal (soluble fiber). Omega-3 2g/day if triglycerides also high.
- **M**: stress management — cortisol drives LDL.
- **Re-test signal**: ApoB ↓ 15–25 mg/dL realistic with diet+exercise in 90 days.

### R-LIP-02 — ApoB ≥ 130 or LDL ≥ 190 — **TIER 1**
- Refer to cardiologist; familial hypercholesterolemia possible. Tessera protocol can run in parallel.

### R-LIP-03 — Elevated triglycerides (TG > 150)
**Root cause**: alcohol, refined carbs, fructose, insulin resistance.
- **N**: drop alcohol · drop refined carbs · drop fructose · increase fatty fish 2×/week or omega-3 supplementation.
- **T**: Zone 2 + resistance (both lower TG).
- **S**: omega-3 (2g EPA+DHA/day).
- **Re-test signal**: TG ↓ 30–50% achievable in 90 days.

### R-LIP-04 — Low HDL (M < 40, F < 50)
**Root cause**: low cardio fitness, low monounsaturated fat intake, smoking, insulin resistance.
- **N**: olive oil, almonds, avocado, fatty fish.
- **T**: Zone 2 5×/week — raises HDL more than any other intervention.
- **S**: niacin only under physician supervision (flushing, liver effects).

### R-LIP-05 — Lp(a) ≥ 50 mg/dL — **TIER 2 (with doctor consult)**
**Root cause**: 90% genetic, lifelong cardiovascular risk amplifier.
- Cannot be moved by lifestyle. Aggressive control of every *other* lipid marker becomes more important.
- Recommend cardiologist consult to discuss aggressive LDL targets (<70) and family screening.

## INFLAMMATION

### R-INF-01 — Elevated hsCRP (1.0–10 mg/L, no acute illness)
**Root cause**: ultra-processed food, seed-oil excess, sleep debt, chronic stress, visceral fat, gut dysbiosis, oral health (gingivitis), low omega-3:6 ratio.
- **N**: anti-inflammatory diet — turmeric (1 tsp/day with black pepper), ginger, berries, leafy greens, fatty fish · drop ultra-processed foods (chips, biscuits, namkeen, packaged sweets, refined oils — palm, rice bran, sunflower).
- **T**: Zone 2 (resolves IL-6 via myokine pathway).
- **R**: 8h sleep non-negotiable.
- **S**: omega-3 (2g EPA+DHA/day), curcumin 500mg × 2/day with food (light-to-moderate evidence). Check dental — periodontitis is a common occult driver.
- **M**: stress reduction — cortisol drives inflammation.
- **Re-test signal**: hsCRP ↓ to <1.0 mg/L typical in 90 days if cause is lifestyle.

### R-INF-02 — hsCRP > 10 mg/L — **TIER 1**
- Likely acute infection or flare. Repeat in 2 weeks. If persistently >10, medical workup (autoimmune, occult infection, malignancy).

### R-INF-03 — Elevated homocysteine (>10 μmol/L)
**Root cause**: B12 / folate / B6 deficiency, MTHFR polymorphism, kidney function, alcohol.
- **S**: B-complex with methylated forms (methylcobalamin 1000mcg, methylfolate 400mcg, P5P 25mg) daily for 12 weeks.
- **N**: leafy greens, eggs, fish.
- **Re-test signal**: homocysteine ↓ 30–40% on methylated B-complex in 12 weeks.

## LIVER

### R-LIV-01 — Elevated ALT or GGT (ALT > 30 M, > 25 F; GGT > 30 M, > 20 F)
**Root cause**: NAFLD (fatty liver), alcohol, medications, viral, autoimmune.
- **N**: drop alcohol entirely for 90 days · drop fructose (no fruit juice, no HFCS) · drop ultra-processed food · 30g protein/meal · time-restricted eating 14:10.
- **T**: Zone 2 + resistance — most evidence-based NAFLD intervention.
- **S**: consider milk thistle (silymarin 200mg × 3/day) if intake confirms heavy past alcohol; light evidence.
- **Re-test signal**: ALT ↓ 30–50% in 90 days if NAFLD-driven.

### R-LIV-02 — ALT or AST ≥ 3× upper limit of normal — **TIER 1**
- Refer to hepatology. Pause supplement recommendations until cleared.

### R-LIV-03 — Low albumin (<3.8 g/dL)
**Root cause**: low protein intake, chronic inflammation, kidney loss.
- **N**: protein audit — target 1.2–1.6g/kg body weight from whole foods.
- **S**: whey isolate post-workout if dietary protein insufficient.
- **Re-test**: ↑ 0.2–0.4 g/dL in 90 days.

## KIDNEY

### R-KID-01 — eGFR 60–89 (mild reduction)
**Root cause**: aging, hypertension, NSAID overuse, diabetes.
- **N**: moderate protein (1.0–1.2 g/kg, not >1.6) · 2.5–3L water/day · sodium <2.3g/day.
- **T**: blood-pressure-focused — Zone 2 + resistance.
- **S**: discontinue daily NSAIDs (ibuprofen, diclofenac) — common occult driver.
- **Re-test**: eGFR ↑ 5–10 mL/min if NSAID-driven.

### R-KID-02 — eGFR < 60 — **TIER 1**
- Nephrology referral. Cap protein at 0.8g/kg until cleared. No creatine, no high-dose Vitamin D without nephrologist sign-off.

---

## THYROID

### R-THY-01 — TSH 4.0–10 (subclinical hypothyroid)
**Root cause**: autoimmune (Hashimoto's — check anti-TPO), iodine deficiency/excess, selenium deficiency, stress.
- **N**: iodine from sea fish 2×/week or 1/4 tsp iodized salt/day · Brazil nuts 2/day (selenium).
- **S**: Vitamin D to >40 ng/mL · selenium 100–200mcg/day for 90 days.
- **Recommend**: endocrinology consult if TSH > 6 or anti-TPO positive; do not start levothyroxine without physician.

### R-THY-02 — TSH > 10 — **TIER 1**
- Endocrinology referral.

## VITAMINS

### R-VIT-01 — Vitamin D < 30 ng/mL
- **S**: 60,000 IU/week for 8 weeks then 2,000 IU/day maintenance, with Vitamin K2 (MK-7) 100mcg/day. Retest at week 12.
- **N**: fatty fish, egg yolks; AM sunlight 15 min/day on bare skin (Indian latitudes).
- Critically common — > 70% of urban Indians are deficient.

### R-VIT-02 — Vitamin D < 20 ng/mL — **TIER 1**
- Recommend physician-supervised loading dose protocol.

### R-VIT-03 — Vitamin B12 < 300 pg/mL
- **N**: eggs (vegetarian) · dairy · sea fish (non-veg) · fortified cereals.
- **S**: methylcobalamin 1000mcg/day sublingual for 12 weeks. Vegetarians/vegans: maintenance 500mcg/day after.
- **Re-test signal**: B12 ↑ 200–400 pg/mL in 12 weeks. If no rise, check intrinsic factor antibodies (pernicious anemia).

### R-VIT-04 — Ferritin < 30 ng/mL (M) or < 15 ng/mL (F)
**Root cause**: low iron intake (vegetarian), heavy menses (F), GI loss.
- **N**: cooked spinach + lemon (Vit C boosts non-heme absorption) · dates · jaggery · whole pulses with citrus · iron pan cooking · don't drink tea/coffee within 1h of iron-rich meals.
- **S**: iron bisglycinate 25–50mg every other day with Vitamin C 500mg (every-other-day dosing absorbs better than daily). For females: rule out menorrhagia with gynecologist.
- **Re-test signal**: ferritin ↑ 30–50 ng/mL in 90 days.

## HORMONES

### R-HOR-01 — Low Testosterone (M total T < 400)
**Root cause**: visceral fat, sleep debt, alcohol, insulin resistance, low resistance training.
- **N**: protein 1.6g/kg · adequate fat (don't drop below 25% calories) · zinc-rich foods (pumpkin seeds, oysters if non-veg).
- **T**: heavy compound resistance training 3×/week (squats, deadlifts, pulls) · short rest periods · 8–12 reps.
- **R**: 8h sleep — testosterone production happens in deep sleep.
- **S**: vitamin D >50 ng/mL · zinc 25mg/day if deficient · magnesium 400mg/day · ashwagandha 600mg/day KSM-66 (moderate evidence for total T ↑).
- **M**: stress reduction (chronic cortisol suppresses T).
- **Re-test signal**: total T ↑ 100–200 ng/dL in 90 days achievable.

### R-HOR-02 — Total T < 250 (M) — **TIER 1**
- Endocrinology consult — rule out primary vs. secondary hypogonadism. Do not start TRT casually.

### R-HOR-03 — Elevated cortisol (AM > 23 μg/dL with chronic-stress intake markers)
- **R**: sleep hygiene · phone out of bedroom · no caffeine after noon.
- **M**: 10-min daily meditation (Headspace/Waking Up) · 5-min slow-breath (6 breaths/min) · weekly nature exposure.
- **N**: stable meals · avoid intermittent fasting beyond 14:10 during high-stress periods.
- **S**: ashwagandha 600mg/day KSM-66 (modest evidence for cortisol reduction).

## SLEEP / RECOVERY (intake-driven, not biomarker)

### R-SLP-01 — Sleep < 7h or wearable sleep efficiency < 85%
- **R**: anchor wake time (same time daily, even weekends) · 15-min AM sunlight (sets circadian rhythm) · caffeine cutoff 10h before bed · no screens 60 min before bed (blue-light glasses if must) · cool room (19–21°C) · weighted blanket.
- **N**: dinner ≥3h before bed · no alcohol within 4h of bed.
- **S**: magnesium glycinate 200–400mg 1h before bed · L-theanine 200mg if anxious sleep onset · low-dose melatonin 0.3–0.5mg ONLY for jet lag, not chronic use.
- **Re-test signal**: HRV ↑ 10–20%, RHR ↓ 3–5 bpm in 90 days.

### R-SLP-02 — Low HRV (< 30 ms 7-day avg from wearable, age-adjusted)
- Compound signal of stress, sleep, training load, alcohol.
- Combine R-SLP-01 + R-INF-01 + cortisol-management protocols.

## TRAINING (intake-driven)

### R-TRN-01 — Sedentary (no structured exercise)
- **T (12-week ramp)**:
  - Weeks 1–2: 30-min walks 5×/week (Zone 2: nose-breathe-able pace) + 2× full-body resistance (bodyweight + light dumbbells, 8 movements, 2 sets each).
  - Weeks 3–6: 45-min walks/cycles 4×/week + 3× resistance + 1× mobility (yoga).
  - Weeks 7–10: add 1× VO₂max (4×4 intervals) · resistance progressed (3 sets, near-failure).
  - Weeks 11–13: 150 min Zone 2 + 2× VO₂max + 3× resistance.
- **Re-test signal**: RHR ↓ 5–10 bpm, HRV ↑ 15–25%, body composition shift, fasting insulin ↓.

### R-TRN-02 — Cardio-only / no resistance
- **T**: introduce 2–3× resistance (full-body, compound lifts). Sarcopenia prevention is the single highest-leverage intervention for healthspan past 35.
- Why: muscle is the largest glucose-disposal organ, drives metabolic rate, predicts mortality.

### R-TRN-03 — Lifter / no Zone 2
- **T**: introduce 90 min Zone 2/week (3× 30-min sessions at 60–70% max HR or MAF). Builds mitochondrial density, lowers ApoB, lowers RHR.

## NUTRITION OVERLAYS (intake-driven)

### R-NUT-VEG — Vegetarian
- Protein gap is the #1 issue. Targets shift: 1.4–1.6g/kg from dal-rice combos, paneer, eggs (if lacto-ovo), Greek yogurt, tofu, tempeh, soy chunks, whey isolate.
- B12 supplementation mandatory (500–1000mcg methylcobalamin).
- Omega-3 from algae (300–500mg DHA), since ALA→EPA conversion is poor.
- Iron — see R-VIT-04.

### R-NUT-INDIAN-FOODS — South / North Indian context
- **Foods to emphasize**: millets (ragi, jowar, bajra), whole pulses, paneer/tofu/eggs, leafy greens (palak, methi, moringa), turmeric+pepper, ghee (moderate), curd, fermented foods (idli/dosa-batter, kanji, pickles), nuts (almonds, walnuts), sea fish 2×/week (non-veg), green tea.
- **Foods to deprioritize**: white rice excess, maida (parathas, naan, biscuits), namkeen, packaged sweets, refined oils (palm, rice bran), sugary drinks (soft drinks, packaged juices, sweet lassi), fried snacks daily, deep-fried street food daily.
- **The dosa question**: a single plain dosa with sambar and a side of egg/paneer bhurji is acceptable; 3 ghee-roast dosas with chutneys and no protein is not. Glycemic load + protein matter more than the food category.

## RULE STACKING — how to combine

In the report's **Personalized Protocol** section, write the user's actual protocol as the *union* of all triggered rules, deduplicated and prioritized:

1. List **Tier 1** findings first, with the physician referral.
2. Pick the **top 3 Tier 2** drivers from the union of triggered rules — these become the "primary targets."
3. Build the 5-domain protocol cards from the union of interventions across all triggered rules. When two rules suggest the same intervention (e.g., omega-3 appears in R-LIP-03 and R-INF-01), surface it once with both rationales.
4. Keep the **90-day plan** focused on ≤ 6 habits total across all 12 weeks. More than that and adherence collapses.
