Important notes

{{disclaimer_sample_note}}

This report is not a medical diagnosis. It is an evidence-based interpretation of a blood panel, a DNA export, wearable telemetry and lifestyle inputs. It does not replace a licensed physician.

Genomics here is research-grade, not diagnostic. Single common variants carry modest effect sizes. A genotype is a probability shift, never a destiny, and never overrides an observed biomarker trend. APOE e4 is disclosed factually with the offer of genetic counseling. Polygenic estimates are largely European-trained and interpreted with South-Asian context where it applies.

Antiaging Labs is a biomarker-moving and healthspan-optimization program. We do not claim to extend lifespan, reverse aging, or cure disease. We move biomarkers and re-test to show what actually moved.

Data and privacy. Personal health data covered by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India). The raw genome file is never stored in the report.
