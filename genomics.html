Why sequence your genome

Four reasons to read it now. Once.

RISK

The biggest single driver of disease risk

For many conditions, your genome explains more of your lifetime risk than any lifestyle factor. It lets us act years earlier, not after diagnosis.

PHARMACOGENOMICS

How your body handles drugs and supplements

Gene variants change how you metabolize statins, caffeine, B-vitamins and more. We use this to choose what actually works for you.

PERSONALIZATION

It sharpens every future protocol

Your genome doesn't change, so you read it once. Every subsequent protocol cycle is more precise because of it.

ANCESTRY CONTEXT

Risk patterns the standard scores miss

Most polygenic risk scores were trained on European-ancestry data. We interpret yours with ancestry-specific risk patterns in mind.
