01 Why we are talking

Longevity in India needs both clinical depth and software scale.

Most longevity clinics worldwide are bottlenecked at the doctor's calendar. Most longevity software companies are bottlenecked at clinical credibility. We can choose to compete and dilute each other, or compose and capture the market together.

Our honest read: Genoryx is doing in Hyderabad what Function Health, Lifeforce, and Chiva-Som are doing globally, a full-stack, doctor-led longevity practice with epigenetic clocks, DEXA, advanced therapies, and real clinical authority. That stack is genuinely hard to replicate. It is your moat.

What we have built is a different shape entirely, an agentic AI pipeline that reads bloodwork, reasons through a versioned clinical rulebook, authors a complete 90-day protocol calibrated to Indian biology, and delivers it through a remote coach. We can serve a thousand patients without adding a doctor. We cannot, however, do a DEXA scan or hand-prescribe a peptide.

The opportunity we want to discuss is what becomes possible when those two stacks compose, a referral pipeline, a step-down maintenance tier, a research dataset, or potentially a joint product. Four concrete models follow in section 04.

04 Four ways to work together

From light-touch to deep partnership.

Listed in order of increasing commitment. We do not assume which one is right , that's what today's conversation is for. Each is a real, viable arrangement on its own.

Model A
Medical advisory & clinical co-signature
Lowest commitment · fastest to start

Your physician becomes Antiaging Labs's medical advisor and co-signs our reports. We get the clinical authority and regulatory backbone that an AI-led product cannot earn on its own. You get equity exposure and an inbound funnel of patients warmed up to longevity thinking.

Genoryx gets
Retainer + 1–2% equity in Antiaging Labs · Inbound referral funnel · Brand co-presence
Antiaging Labs gets
Clinical co-signature on reports · Medical-director credibility · Regulatory cover
Time to launch
2–4 weeks from agreement
Model B
Bi-directional referral pipeline
Highest signal value · lowest friction

Antiaging Labs funnels clients with Tier-1 findings or advanced-diagnostic needs up to Genoryx, DEXA, epigenetic clock, hormone therapy, suspected pathology. Genoryx funnels graduated post-protocol patients down to Antiaging Labs for ongoing 90-day maintenance cycles. Revenue share both directions.

Genoryx gets
Pre-qualified, biology-screened leads · 60–90 day patient retention loop
Antiaging Labs gets
Graduated maintenance patients · Clinical escalation path · Real-world referral data
Revenue share
15–20% on referred patients, either direction · Tracked monthly
Model C
"Genoryx Digital", white-label powered by Antiaging Labs
Highest revenue upside · deeper commitment

A Genoryx-branded digital product that uses Antiaging Labs's full AI engine under the hood. Lets Genoryx reach mass-premium patients across India without building software, hiring engineers, or sacrificing the in-clinic brand. We handle the tech; you handle the clinical voice and regulatory presence.

Genoryx gets
A digital revenue stream · National reach · Asset-light expansion
Antiaging Labs gets
Distribution at scale · Clinical brand stamp · Multi-year contract revenue
Revenue split
~50/50 on Genoryx Digital subscriptions · Operationally clean
Model D
Joint research & longitudinal cohort
Long-term moat for both sides

India has no longitudinal longevity dataset that anyone has published on. Between Genoryx's deep-panel patients and Antiaging Labs's volume cohort, we can build one. Co-author the first South Asian aging-biology paper. Both brands lift academically; Antiaging Labs gets the dataset; Genoryx gets the publication credit and a defensible "we studied this" position.

Genoryx gets
Academic credibility · Co-authored paper · Long-term research presence

05 What we'd start with, a 60-day pilot

No contracts. No marketing. Just outcomes.

The fastest way for both sides to see if this works is to run a small, time-boxed pilot of Model B, bidirectional referrals, for 60 days. Zero financial commitment from Genoryx until both sides see the data.

A 60-day proof-of-collaboration
Five patients each way. Sixty days. One review.
If it works, we sign. If it doesn't, we both walk, with data, not opinions.
DAYS 1–14
Patient identification
Genoryx flags 5 graduated patients for Antiaging Labs maintenance. Antiaging Labs flags 5 clients with Tier-1 findings for Genoryx workup.
DAYS 15–45
Cross-care delivery
Each side handles their referred cohort. Shared private dashboard tracks handoffs, outcomes, and patient feedback.
DAYS 46–55
Outcome assessment
Joint review of biomarker deltas, patient NPS, clinical concordance, operational friction points.
DAYS 56–60
Decision point
Renew & scale to a formal Model B agreement, or close with a clean break and learnings on both sides.
Zero cost to Genoryx during the pilot. Patient consent & data-handling protocols signed before any referral moves. Both sides retain unilateral right to terminate at any time.

07 Open questions for Genoryx

Things we genuinely don't know, and would value your view on.

A partnership only works if both sides answer the hard questions in the first conversation. These are the ones we'd ask if we had thirty minutes of your time.

  1. What's your patient acquisition cost, and where do most of them come from today?
  2. What's your real bottleneck right now, patient volume, doctor hours, or protocol authoring time?
  3. What happens to a patient after their intensive 6-month protocol ends? Is there a maintenance gap?
  4. Are you publishing anything, papers, longitudinal observations, anything you'd want academic standing on?
  5. What's the next category you'd want to build in but can't because of bandwidth or capital?
  6. What would a Antiaging Labs report need to contain for you to feel comfortable putting your physician's signature on it?
