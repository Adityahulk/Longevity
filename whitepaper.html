03 · The computational core

Rigorous mathematical formulations

To convert raw multi-modal inputs into an actionable software moat, Antiaging Labs implements two core mathematical engines to compute biological age and integrate high-dimensional omic streams.

Engine A / the Levine proportional hazards and PhenoAge transformation

To measure systemic phenotypic aging from the clinical hardware layer, the platform implements the framework published by Dr. Morgan Levine, trained via a multivariate Cox proportional hazards model on NHANES datasets.

Step 1 · linear predictor (xb)
$$ \begin{aligned} xb = -19.907 \;&- 0.0336\,\text{Alb} + 0.0095\,\text{Creat} + 0.1953\,\text{Glucose} + 0.0954\ln(\text{hsCRP}) \\ &- 0.0120\,\text{Lymph}\% + 0.0268\,\text{MCV} + 0.3306\,\text{RDW} + 0.00188\,\text{ALP} \\ &+ 0.0554\,\text{WBC} + 0.0804\,CA \end{aligned} $$

The software ingests nine core clinical biomarkers and chronological age ($CA$) into a deterministic linear combination.

Step 2 · composite 10-year mortality score (M)
$$ M = 1 - \exp\!\left( \frac{-\exp(xb)\,\bigl(\exp(120\gamma) - 1\bigr)}{\gamma} \right) $$

Utilizing a Gompertz proportional-hazards parameterization, the linear predictor is converted into an explicit probability-risk curve, where the scaling constant is fixed by geroscience consensus at γ = 0.0076927.

Step 3 · phenotypic biological age
$$ \text{PhenoAge} = 141.50225 + \frac{\ln\!\bigl(-0.00553\,\ln(1 - M)\bigr)}{0.090165} $$

The mortality score is transformed back into an intuitive age metric via a reverse Gompertz distribution. A positive delta ($\text{PhenoAge} - CA > 0$) signals accelerated biological aging, serving as the baseline metric that triggers corrective protocol overrides.

Engine B / the Klemera-Doubal method for multi-omic integration

When the system integrates non-blood layers, such as MRI structural densities, proteomic panels and metabolomic arrays, the standard PhenoAge linear equation faces collinearity and error-propagation limits. The platform deploys the Klemera-Doubal algorithm, which minimizes the sum of squared distances between individual biomarker regression lines in a multi-dimensional space, anchored by chronological age.

KDM biological age across m biomarkers
$$ BA_{KDM} = \frac{\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{m} \left( \frac{x_j - q_j}{k_j} \right)\!\left( \frac{k_j^2}{s_j^2} \right) + \frac{CA}{s_{CA}^2}}{\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{m} \left( \frac{k_j^2}{s_j^2} \right) + \frac{1}{s_{CA}^2}} $$
  • $x_j$ · the real-time observed value of biomarker $j$ (e.g. direct proteomic volume or visceral fat mass from a DEXA scan).
  • $k_j$ · the slope of biomarker $j$ regressed on chronological age in a healthy reference population.
  • $q_j$ · the intercept of biomarker $j$ regressed on chronological age.
  • $s_j$ · the standard deviation of the residuals for biomarker $j$ from the population regression.
  • $s_{CA}$ · the variance of chronological age in the reference population, acting as an intentional mathematical stabilizer.
