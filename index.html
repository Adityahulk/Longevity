The missing layer

Performance data is useful. It is not the whole picture.

Pace, load, sleep scores and body weight describe outputs. They do not reliably show the metabolic and physiological constraints underneath them.

01 / METABOLIC

Glucose can look fine while insulin is already working too hard.

Fasting insulin, HbA1c and triglyceride patterns add context that a single glucose value misses.

02 / CARDIO

Fitness does not automatically remove lipid risk.

ApoB, Lp(a) and related markers can reveal risk that training volume or LDL-C alone cannot settle.

03 / RECOVERY

Low energy is not always a motivation problem.

Iron status, thyroid context, inflammation and nutrient deficiencies can change how training and recovery feel.

The 90-day system

One baseline. One focused protocol. One comparable retest.

The work is structured as a feedback loop, not a one-off health score.

01 / Measure

Build the baseline

Bloodwork is combined with medical history, current medications, family history, diet, training, sleep, stress and relevant wearable context.

02 / Interpret

Prioritize the signal

Findings are linked to published evidence, checked for interactions and contraindications, and organized around the few constraints most worth addressing first.

03 / Act

Run a realistic protocol

Nutrition, training, recovery and supplements are translated into specific actions that fit the client’s schedule, preferences and starting capacity.

04 / Re-measure

Review the response

The relevant panel is repeated under comparable conditions. Marker-level changes, adherence and measurement limitations are reviewed together.

Results, documented properly

A result without context is marketing, not evidence.

Tessera case studies will only publish consented outcomes with baseline context, intervention dates, comparable test conditions, marker-level changes and limitations.

Publication standard

Verified case studies are being prepared for publication.

Until the supporting records and publication consent are complete, illustrative report data remains clearly labelled and is never presented as a client outcome.

  • Written consent and anonymization status
  • Baseline and retest dates
  • Comparable collection conditions
  • Intervention and adherence context
  • Limitations and non-responsive markers

Fit and exclusions

Built for active people who want a better feedback loop.

The consultation exists partly to identify when Tessera is not the right service.

A useful fit

  • You already train or track recovery and want deeper biological context.
  • You are willing to do bloodwork, follow a structured protocol and retest.
  • You prefer measurable priorities over supplement trends and generic optimization advice.
  • You are generally stable enough for a coached lifestyle program.

Not what Tessera does

  • Emergency care, diagnosis or primary management of active disease.
  • Starting, stopping or adjusting prescription medication.
  • Rapid weight-loss or aesthetic transformation programs.
  • Guaranteed biomarker improvement, lifespan extension or biological-age reversal.
