Why we exist

Aging shows up in your biology years before symptoms.

A standard checkup is built to catch illness once it arrives. The signals that predict how you'll age, in your blood, your genome, your body composition and your arteries, drift quietly long before that. Almost no one is reading them together, least of all for South Asian bodies.

01 / METABOLIC

The early markers are missed

Insulin, ApoB and inflammation move years before glucose or cholesterol flag anything. Standard panels stop too early.

02 / CALIBRATION

Built for other bodies

Reference ranges and longevity products were trained on populations that aren't South Asian, who develop metabolic risk earlier, at lower BMI.

03 / FEEDBACK

No before and after

Even good advice has no measured loop. You never actually learn what worked for your own biology.
