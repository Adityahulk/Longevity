# Tessera Content Strategy — The Educator Engine

> **Positioning:** Tessera's founders are the public educators who translate cutting-edge aging & biomarker science into what to actually do. Not influencers. Not lifestyle gurus. The credible translators. Authority is the moat; trust is the conversion mechanism.

> **The one rule:** every piece teaches something genuinely useful even if the reader never buys. If a post only makes sense as marketing, don't publish it.

---

## Why this, why now

- Clients won't come from cold website traffic today. They come from **trust built over time** + warm network + partnerships. Content is the trust-at-scale engine.
- It finds the **5% who already understand longevity** (and can't find an SA-calibrated product) and educates the next 15% who can be moved.
- It compounds: an essay written once works for years. SEO + shareability + authority all accrue.
- It feeds the bigger story — the open engine, the transparency, the dataset — to investors and partners who read before they meet you.

---

## The hub

- **Home:** on-site at `/learn` ("Field Notes"). We own the audience and the SEO. Newsletter capture layered on.
- **Foundations reference:** `/research` (the deep science page) — cornerstone content the field notes build on.
- **Cadence:** **1 flagship essay/week**, on-site first. Then repurpose. **NOT daily.** Consistency beats volume. Four good pieces a week (essay + repurposed social) for six months beats daily slop that fizzles in three weeks.

## Voice guide (educator, not influencer)

**Do**
- Translate real science: name the study/mechanism, then say what it means for the reader's bloodwork.
- Tag evidence honestly (E1 strong / E2 moderate / speculative). Admit error bars and limits — anti-hype *is* the trust play.
- Plain English. Short sentences. One idea per paragraph. Concrete numbers.
- End with the actionable version: "here's what to actually do / look at."
- Be willing to say "we don't know yet" and "this is oversold."

**Don't**
- No hype ("reverse your age 10 years!"), no fear-mongering, no supplement affiliate links.
- No "queen energy" / no patronizing / no guru tone.
- No claiming certainty the evidence doesn't support.
- No emojis in long-form. No exclamation points.

**Litmus test:** would a skeptical doctor or a data-literate founder nod, or roll their eyes? Write for the nod.

## The 6 content pillars

1. **Reference-range literacy** — "normal vs optimal," what your report hides, SA-calibration gap. *(Sharpest, most shareable, most on-brand.)*
2. **Biomarker explainers** — one marker per piece: ferritin, fasting insulin, ApoB, vitamin D, TSH, hs-CRP, HbA1c, Lp(a).
3. **Intervention science** — what actually moves markers, by evidence tier: nutrition, training, sleep, supplements.
4. **Biological age & clocks** — PhenoAge, epigenetic clocks, how to read a bio-age number honestly.
5. **The open engine / build-in-public** — why we publish the rulebook, how agentic clinical reasoning works, what we're learning. *(Founder's lane; doubles as investor/partner credibility.)*
6. **South Asian calibration** — why Indian bodies need different ranges, MASALA-study-type findings, earlier metabolic risk.

## Repurpose ladder (1 essay → 7+ assets)

Every flagship essay becomes:
- **1 LinkedIn post** (founder voice; the thesis + 1 surprising stat + link) — pillar 5 pieces especially.
- **1 Instagram carousel** (6–8 slides; the essay's core argument, designed clean — no stock photos).
- **1 X/Twitter thread** (the argument in 6–10 tweets).
- **3–5 short snippets** (single insight + a hook, for IG/LinkedIn reposts and WhatsApp shares).
- **1 newsletter send** (the essay + a short "what we're learning" note).

Batch production: **one focused half-day per week** writes the essay + drafts all repurposed assets. Don't create daily from scratch.

Two-person formats (one explains the science, the other the human stakes) are the highest-trust content — lead with a few of those.

## Metrics that matter (ignore the rest)

- **Saves / shares** per post (intent signal) — optimize for this, not likes.
- **Newsletter open rate** (target >40%; a small engaged list beats a big dead one).
- **DM → consultation-call** conversion.
- **"How did you find us?"** on every consult — content attribution.
- **Essay read-through + scroll depth** on `/learn`.

Vanity metrics (follower count, raw reach): track quarterly for trend, never optimize for.

---

## Operating cadence

- **Weekly:** 1 flagship essay published + repurposed across channels (one batch half-day).
- **Near-daily:** lightweight stories/reposts (30 sec, not 3 hours) to stay present.
- **Monthly:** review metrics, prune what's not landing, double down on the pillar that converts.
- **Never:** daily long-form production. That's the burnout path that kills the engine in week 3.
