Lever 01 · Training Lead

Build the aerobic base you never had

Right now you do under 30 minutes of structured aerobic work a week while sitting most of the day (ACWR 0.58, VO2max drifting down, ~5,000 steps). That single gap is why your HDL is low, your insulin is creeping, and your energy is gone. Zone-2 is the top lever for all three at once, and for an APOE e4 carrier it is a brain lever too. You are exercise-poor, not over-trained, so the job is to add a base, built so it survives constant travel.

Weekly structure

  • Zone-2 cardio: 150 min/week, in 3-4 sessions of 35-45 min at a conversational pace (heart rate ~120-135, your Whoop confirms the zone). Incline treadmill, cycle, brisk walk, or any hotel-gym cardio. On Bangalore or Delhi high-AQI days, keep it indoors with filtered air.
  • Resistance: 2 full-body sessions/week (squat, hinge, push, pull, carry). Muscle is your largest glucose sink, this is half the insulin fix. Dumbbell or bodyweight versions travel with you.
  • Steps to 8,000/day, up from ~5,000. Walking meetings and calls taken on foot do most of the work.
  • One short VO2max session/week, added only once the base is in (from week 7): 4-6 x 1 min hard with full recoveries.
  • One true recovery day, picked by the Whoop trend, walk and mobility only.

The 12-week progression

  • Weeks 1-2, install the base: 2 Zone-2 (30 min) + 2 strength + hit 8k steps. No intensity, just consistency and the sleep and caffeine rules.
  • Weeks 3-6, build: 3 Zone-2 (40 min) + 2 strength. Watch resting HR start to fall and energy return. Book the lipid appointment.
  • Weeks 7-10, progress: 3-4 Zone-2 + the first VO2max blocks once recovery is solid. Week 8 lighter (travel-heavy). Resistance loads progress.
  • Weeks 11-13, consolidate: hold the structure. No heavy training in the 48 hours before the Day-90 retest, it can skew the panel.

Your ACTN3 is a mixed power/endurance profile, a tie-breaker only, the Zone-2 priority stands. Your BDNF Met variant means aerobic work and sleep are doubly worthwhile for the brain. Evidence: Zone-2 for VO2max, HDL and insulin sensitivity [E1]; resistance for glucose disposal [E1]; aerobic fitness and cognition [E2].
