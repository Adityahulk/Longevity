08 · The protocol

Five levers, tuned to you.

Genome-informed and wearable-driven. The goal of 90 days is not to do everything, it is to install six habits deeply enough that they do not need willpower.

Pillar 1

Drive ApoB under 70

Zone-2 cardio 150 min a week, soluble fiber 10g a day, saturated to unsaturated fat swap, omega-3, and the physician conversation about a statin. One change set, your biggest genetic risk.

Targets by Day 90: ApoB 105 to under 85 (toward under 70 with pharmacotherapy) · LDL 142 to under 115 · HDL 38 to 44+ · AIP 0.64 to 0.4
Pillar 2

Recover as hard as he works

Sleep 6 to 7.5 hours, caffeine cutoff 10:00 (slow metabolizer), one true recovery day, daily breathwork. The lever behind the energy and focus he came in for.

Targets by Day 90: HRV 32 back toward 41 · RHR 62 to 58 · cortisol 18 to 14 · deep sleep 11% to 16% · testosterone 420 to 480+
Pillar 3

Reverse the early insulin resistance

Zone-2 plus two resistance sessions, protein-forward lower-glycemic meals that survive travel, fewer late dinners and drinks. Visceral fat is the common denominator.

Targets by Day 90: HOMA-IR 2.91 to under 2.0 · glucose 100 to under 95 · TG 165 to under 120 · ALT 48 to under 35 · waist 96 to under 92
Lever 01 · Recovery & Sleep Lead

The cheapest win he is not taking

  • Lights-out 23:00, phone charges outside the bedroom (he reads in bed now). Wake fixed at 06:45. Target 7 to 7.5 hours.
  • Caffeine cutoff 10:00, two cups maximum, because he clears it slowly. Replace the 3pm coffee with a 10-min daylight walk.
  • One true recovery day a week, chosen by the Whoop trend. Magnesium glycinate in the evening.
  • Travel rule: on red-eye nights, hydrate, keep caffeine before 10:00 local, daylight on landing, no training next day.
Lever 02 · Nutrition Lead

Two structural moves, built for travel

  • Shift fat quality (Mediterranean, per APOE e4): olive and mustard oil over ghee, cut the fried orders, halve the after-dinner dessert.
  • Add 1 tbsp psyllium daily plus oats and chia. The veg-light, travel-proof ApoB fiber move.
  • Protein at every meal (eggs, dahi, chicken, fish, paneer, dal) for satiety and glucose control.
  • Order smarter: grilled over fried, double the dal and sabzi, less rice, skip the naan-and-dessert combo. Drinks to two a week.
Lever 03 · Training Lead

Build the aerobic base he is missing

  • Zone-2: 3 to 4 sessions of 35 to 45 min a week (HR ~120 to 135), confirmed by the Whoop. This is the top lever for HDL, ApoB, insulin and his energy.
  • Resistance: 2 full-body sessions a week, portable versions for travel. Muscle is his glucose sink.
  • Steps to 8,000 a day with walking meetings. One short VO2max session added once the base is in.
Lever 04 · Supplements

Six targeted items, not a stack

  • Omega-3 2g EPA+DHA, psyllium 1 tbsp, vitamin D3 2000 IU with K2, magnesium glycinate PM, creatine 3 to 5g.
  • Methylfolate plus methyl-B12 (not folic acid), chosen by the MTHFR result and the measured homocysteine.
  • A statin stays a physician decision, with the SLCO1B1 advisory attached.
Lever 05 · Mind & Wellness

Down-regulate a real, situational stress load

  • 10 min of breathwork daily (physiological sigh or box breathing), matched to his COMT profile.
  • A genuine offline block on the recovery day, walking meetings to fold movement into work.
