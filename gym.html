08 Open questions for you

Things we'd want to understand before we go further.

A partnership only works if both sides answer the honest questions in the first meeting. These are the ones we'd ask if we had thirty minutes with you.

  1. What's your real year-one to year-two retention rate — and what do you blame the drop on?
  2. What percentage of your members would you describe as "results-hungry premium" — the ones quietly looking for transformation programs beyond what the gym alone can deliver?
  3. How are your trainers compensated today — flat, commission, or hybrid? The structure shapes how we'd integrate them into the program.
  4. Have you tried any wellness or nutrition partnerships before, and what specifically didn't work?
  5. If we ran a 60-day pilot, what would convince you it's working — member NPS, renewal data, trainer feedback, all three?
  6. What's the part of your member journey you wish you could fix tomorrow but haven't?
