Whole-body MRI

One scan. A look at the whole body.

A radiation-free MRI screens major organs and regions in a single session, surfacing things that have no early symptoms and never show up in blood.

EARLY DETECTION

Catches the silent stuff

Many serious conditions grow for years without symptoms. Imaging is how you find them while they're still small and addressable.

NO RADIATION

Safe enough to repeat

MRI uses magnetic fields, not ionizing radiation, so it's appropriate for proactive, repeatable screening over time.

WHOLE BODY

Brain to pelvis in one session

Major organs and regions reviewed together by a radiologist, then summarized for you in plain language.

DEXA body composition

The number on the scale lies. This doesn't.

A DEXA scan separates fat, muscle and bone, and pinpoints visceral fat, the dangerous fat around your organs that drives metabolic risk even at lower body weights.

  • Visceral fat quantified, not guessed
  • Lean muscle mass by region, your true baseline
  • Bone mineral density, catching osteopenia early
  • A precise baseline to measure your protocol against

How we handle it

Imaging, done honestly.

We don't own scanners, and we don't add imaging by default. It's recommended only when it will change your plan, run through accredited partner centers, and read into the same record as your bloodwork and genome.

01 · INDICATED

Only when it helps

We suggest imaging when your bloodwork, history or genome point to a reason, not as a routine upsell.

02 · PARTNER

Accredited centers

Scans are performed at established partner imaging centers in select cities, read by qualified radiologists.

03 · INTEGRATED

Read into one record

Findings feed the same engine as your blood and genome, so your protocol reflects the full picture.
