PhenoAge
Markers tagged PhenoAge are the 9 inputs used to calculate your biological age score using the Levine et al. 2018 (Nature Medicine) validated model. All 9 are captured within this panel from a single fasted blood draw.
Collection Instructions — For Lab
Fasting
10–12 hours minimum. Water allowed. No food, coffee, or supplements.
Timing
7–10 AM strictly. Testosterone and cortisol peak in this window.
hsCRP Assay
High-sensitivity method mandatory. Standard CRP not accepted.
Fasting Insulin
Chemiluminescence method preferred for accuracy.
CBC Differential
5-part differential required. 3-part not sufficient for this panel.
Female Hormones
Note menstrual cycle day on report. Day 2–5 preferred for FSH/LH.
Report Format
PDF soft copy, all individual values itemized, within 48 hours of draw.
Sample Type
EDTA tube (CBC, HbA1c) + SST serum tubes + fluoride tube (FBS). Single venipuncture.
Total Draw
Approximately 20–25 mL total across 3–4 tubes. Standard volume.
Single venipuncture. One sitting. ~5 minutes at collection.
