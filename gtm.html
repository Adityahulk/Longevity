Part I — What we sell & who we sell it to

What we sell (in one line)

A deep, personalized health program that combines a person's blood work, DNA, and wearable data into one reasoning engine — then turns it into a specific protocol (diet, training, recovery, supplements) and re-tests to prove what actually moved. One program, premium-priced.

What makes us different (you'll need this constantly)

Most health products give you a snapshot: a blood test, a nice dashboard, generic advice. Ours fuses three data layers (blood + DNA + wearable) and reasons across them — and every recommendation is traceable to why it was made. Competitors (e.g. Superpower in the US) do blood + wearable monitoring only; nobody fuses the genome in the way we do. That depth is the whole pitch.

Guardrails (do not drift from these)

  • We are not a cheap blood-test app. Price is not our selling point; depth and the ongoing loop are. Never pitch "we're cheaper."
  • No cold blasting. First customers come from trust, not volume. Quality over quantity, always.
  • No discounting our way in. Premium buyers value access and status, not a discount. (More in the offer section.)
  • Only claims we can back. We move markers and re-test; we don't promise miracles or make "anti-aging" claims. If a line would make a careful doctor wince, cut it.

Who exactly to target (the ICP)

The health-anxious optimizer with a trigger.

Qualify IN if they are:

  • Roughly 30–48, high-earning (founder, tech/finance professional, doctor, or serious athlete).
  • Already wearing an Oura / Whoop / Apple Watch — data-literate, frustrated that "your reports are normal" tells them nothing.
  • Carrying a present-tense trigger: a personal health scare, a bad test result, or a parent's diagnosis. This urgency is the single best predictor of who pays.
  • Reachable warm — through the founder or someone who trusts him.

Disqualify OUT (don't waste a consult on them):

  • Wants fast weight loss or looks in 30 days.
  • Wants supplements without changing behaviour.
  • Has an active medical condition that needs a doctor's primary care.
  • Won't do blood work or share wearable data.
  • Just "curious," no urgency.

Channels, best first

  1. Athletic communities — our best starting channel. The founder is a national Muay Thai champion and a marathon runner. That earns him real, authentic credibility inside serious gyms and running/triathlon/CrossFit clubs — which are packed with high-earning, wearable-obsessed optimizers. He's one of them, not a marketer. Start sourcing here.
  2. Closest peers who fit the ICP — people who already talk to the founder about health, training, sleep. Warmest, fastest to close.
  3. IIITA alumni (the founder's college network) — data-literate, high-earning, trust the shared background.
  4. Professional network — respectful peer contacts only.
  5. AI-research & past-startup contacts — many appreciate the tech; some are in the US diaspora.
  6. Diaspora — anyone from the groups above who moved to the US.

Always source warmest-first. Early wins create testimonials and social proof, which make every later message land easier.

Your daily target: prep and get 4–6 personalized messages ready for the founder each weekday, and keep the calendar full of consults. That pace reaches 60–75 messages in about three weeks.

The consult is a live demo, not a pitch

This is where we win. On the call, the founder shows the engine working on the person's own data instead of describing it.

  • When you book someone, ask them to bring an existing health report or wearable export. Put it in the founder's brief.
  • The founder produces one insight they've never heard — e.g. "Your HRV's been dropping for three weeks, and because of how you process caffeine, here's the exact reason and the one change to make." A snapshot tool literally cannot say that line. That moment is the sale.

Consult structure (30 min): their story & trigger (5) → live reading of their data (12) → the program, price stated plainly (5) → the founding offer (5) → close or dated next step (3).

The founding offer

Keep the price near full (starts at ₹1,10,000/year). Win on status and access, not discount:

  • Founding-member rate locked for life — they never pay a future price rise.
  • Direct founder access — he personally runs their first cycle.
  • Concierge onboarding — we handle the blood draw and DNA logistics for them.
  • A voice in the product — their feedback shapes it.

In return, the founder asks for three things, explicitly:

  1. Commitment — the 12-month program.
  2. A testimonial with a number, once their re-test shows movement (e.g. "ApoB down 22% in 10 weeks").
  3. One warm referral to someone who fits the ICP.

Part III — Sprint plan (week by week)

Sprints are one week, Mon–Sun. Dates below are anchored to a start of Mon 6 Jul 2026 — shift them to your real start date. Move fast; the whole plan is ~4 weeks to 10 members.

Sprint 0 — Prep (Mon–Wed, 6–8 Jul)

Goal: everything ready to send the first message and hold the first consult.

  • Build the target list — 60–75 names across the channels above, in the tracker (Part IV). One line each: name · channel · warmth · personalization hook.
  • Consult logistics ready — Calendly link live, video call set up, the 30-min structure printed for the founder.
  • Message templates — 4 short variants (gym/running · IIITA · peer · diaspora). Personalize per person at send time.
  • Founding-offer one-pager — the perks + the three asks, to send after a good call.
  • Collection process confirmed — India concierge draw path + the US self-serve steps, so a "yes" never stalls on logistics.

Done when: 60+ qualified names logged, Calendly tested end-to-end, the founder has rehearsed one full mock consult out loud.

Sprint 1 — Source & first wave (Thu 9 – Wed 15 Jul)

Goal: outreach running; consults landing on the calendar.

  • Your quota: 5–6 personalized messages prepped for the founder each weekday (~30–35 for the week), warmest channels first (athletic + closest peers).
  • Personalize the first line every time — a real detail proves it's them, not a blast. If there's no detail, find one before it goes out.
  • One ask only: "want me to run your numbers on a quick call?"
  • The moment someone replies yes, send the Calendly link the same day.
  • Confirm every booked consult on booking + a day-before reminder (no-shows are our #1 leak).

Done when: ~30 messages sent, 8–10 consults booked, first 2–3 held.

Sprint 2 — Consults & first closes (Thu 16 – Wed 22 Jul)

Goal: turn conversations into paying members; get the first testimonials.

  • Keep sourcing at a lighter pace (3–4/day, ~20 more) — the pipeline can't run dry while the founder closes.
  • Support 6–8 consults — send the founder a one-line brief on each person beforehand (their trigger, their hook, what data they're bringing).
  • Target: first 3–4 members closed.
  • The moment someone says yes: kick off their blood + DNA onboarding that same week, and the founder asks for a referral while they're excited.

Done when: 3–4 paid, ~5 more consults booked, first referrals logged.

Sprint 3 — Close to 10 & harvest referrals (Thu 23 – Wed 29 Jul)

Goal: reach double digits; turn early members into a referral engine.

  • Support the remaining consults — referral-sourced leads close highest, prioritize them.
  • Target: 8–10 total members.
  • Referral push: for every closed member, the founder asks a specific question — "who's the one person you know who'd get the most from this?" Specific beats "anyone come to mind?"
  • Start collecting early reactions in the member's own words, even before re-tests land.

Done when: 8–10 paid members, ≥3 referrals in the pipeline, ≥2 quotable reactions.

Sprint 4 — Proof & momentum (Thu 30 Jul – Tue 5 Aug)

Goal: consolidate the wins and set up the next wave.

  • Lock the numbers: members closed, revenue, close rate, time-from-zero. Put them in a simple one-page traction summary for the founder.
  • Get 1–2 testimonials — ideally one with an early number; if re-tests haven't landed, use the sharpest "this showed me something no checkup did" quote.
  • Refill the pipeline for the next cohort from the referrals collected.
  • Retro: what channel converted best, where leads leaked, what to change next round.

Done when: a clean traction summary exists, ≥1 testimonial captured, and the next cohort's list is started.

Part IV — How you track & run the day-to-day

The tracker (one row per person)

name · channel · warmth (hot/warm/cool) · personalization hook · status · next action · next-action date · notes

Status flow: to-reach → reached → replied → booked → held → won / lost / nurture

Every person always has a next action and a date. A lead with no next step is a dropped lead.

Daily (about 2 hours)

  • Prep the day's personalized messages and hand them to the founder.
  • Confirm and remind tomorrow's consults.
  • Move every "replied" toward a booking; update the tracker on everything.

Part VI — After the first 10

The first 10 aren't the finish line — they're the proof and the seed of the flywheel:

  more community & doctor introductions → diaspora → wider reach over time

Only once the loop is proven and members are seeing results do we widen — a lighter-tier product, content as a credibility layer, and repeatable partner channels. Not yet. Right now the entire job is: 10 warm, deep, delighted customers.

One-paragraph summary

Sell the deep, multi-layer health program to the founder's warmest, highest-urgency network — athletic communities and close peers first, then college and professional contacts, then the US diaspora — one consult at a time, where the consult is a live demo of depth no snapshot tool can match. You power the engine (list, personalization, scheduling, reminders, tracking, testimonials); the founder sends the warm messages, runs the consults, and closes. About 60–75 warm messages → ~22 consults → 10 founding members, won on status and access rather than discount, each asked for a testimonial and a referral. Run it in weekly sprints, keep the pipeline full while closing, and get to 10 delighted, paying customers.
