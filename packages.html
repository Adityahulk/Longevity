Why it's built this way

Buy a data type once. It improves every protocol after.

Your genome doesn't change, so you read it once and it personalizes every future cycle. Your wearable streams continuously, so each retest is richer than the last. Blood is the moving picture you re-measure every 90 days. Because everything lands in one record, the engine reasons over your full history, not a single snapshot. This is why Tessera is a record and an engine, not just a test.
