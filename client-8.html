Lever 01 · Nutrition Lead

Eat for insulin sensitivity

Your lipids are already good, so this is not about cutting fat. It is about steadying insulin and refilling nutrients.

The insulin work

  • Protein-forward meals with each meal, especially a protein-rich breakfast, to blunt glucose and insulin spikes.
  • More fibre (vegetables, legumes, whole grains) and fewer refined carbs and sugary drinks, the main driver of high fasting insulin.
  • A 10 to 15 minute walk after meals, a simple, powerful lever on post-meal glucose.

The nutrient work

  • Folate: leafy greens, legumes, citrus. Priority if pregnancy is possible.
  • Iron: iron-rich foods paired with vitamin C to help absorption; keep dairy and tea away from iron-rich meals.
Provisional until we have your typical meals, vegetarian status, weight and waist, and whether you are planning a pregnancy.
Lever 02 · Training

Build muscle, walk often

Exercise is the most direct non-food lever on insulin resistance, and it lifts a low HDL too.

  • Strength training, 2 to 3 sessions/week. More muscle means more places to store glucose, which lowers insulin directly.
  • Zone-2 cardio, 150 min/week (a brisk pace where you can still talk), which improves insulin sensitivity and raises HDL.
  • Daily steps and post-meal walks as an easy baseline.
Provisional until we know your current activity, time available and any injuries.
