The program

Measure, intervene, re-measure.

Most health products stop at measurement. The program closes the loop: a baseline, a protocol calibrated to your numbers, and a retest that shows what actually moved. Here is what that involves, who it's for, and what you receive.

01 · The four levers

Every protocol balances four interventions.

No single master variable. Sleep matters, but so does ApoB. Training matters, but so does cortisol. The protocol moves all four, in proportion to what your body needs most.

NUTRITION

Nutrition, prescribed

Macro targets, meal timing, protein distribution and fiber thresholds, built from your insulin sensitivity, lipid panel and body composition. Not a diet. A specification.

TRAINING

Training, structured

Zone-2 for mitochondrial density, VO2-max work for aerobic capacity, resistance training for strength and bone. Three distinct stimuli, all measurable.

RECOVERY

Recovery, monitored

Sleep architecture, HRV, resting heart rate and stress markers. We use wearable data to make sure the protocol is building you up, not breaking you down.

SUPPLEMENTS

Supplements, audited

We start by removing what you don't need, then add only what your bloodwork says is missing. Most people end with a smaller, cheaper, more effective stack. No affiliate fees, ever.

Every protocol is reviewed by our medical advisor before it reaches you. We work alongside your doctor, not in place of them.

02 · Who it's for

Built for the data-literate. Not the wellness-curious.

We're upfront about who this works for and who it doesn't. The program is rigorous, asks for real behaviour change, and rewards people who like reading their own data. It isn't a magic pill, and we won't sell you one.

THIS IS FOR YOU IF
  • You're roughly 28 to 55, generally healthy, and want to optimize, not just maintain.
  • You already track something (sleep, training, food) and want to do it better.
  • "Your bloodwork looks normal" has stopped feeling reassuring.
  • You'll commit a few hours a week to training, recovery and adherence.
  • You want a system, not motivation.
THIS IS NOT FOR YOU IF
  • You want fast weight loss or aesthetic results in 30 days.
  • You want supplements without any behaviour change.
  • You have an active condition that needs primary clinical management.
  • You're not willing to do bloodwork or share wearable data.
  • You expect "anti-aging" claims. We don't make them. We move markers and re-test.

04 · Questions

More, answered honestly.

Does this work for women in their 30s and 40s?

Yes. The panel includes a complete sex-hormone profile and accounts for cycle timing on bloodwork. The protocol adjusts for menstrual phase and hormone-related metabolic shifts. We don't apply male-default protocols to women.

Can I do this if I'm already on medication?

In most cases, yes. Our medical advisor reviews your current medications before designing the protocol. We never recommend stopping or changing a prescription. For some active conditions we may suggest stabilizing with your physician first. The free consultation is partly to figure this out.

How much time does it actually take?

A home blood draw, a short intake call, and a few hours a week of training and habits that mostly fit into your existing life. The protocol is designed around your constraints, not a fantasy schedule.

What if my markers don't move?

We re-test either way and keep working a stubborn marker at no extra cost. We'll be honest about why: some markers are slow movers biologically, and some haven't moved because of adherence rather than biology.
