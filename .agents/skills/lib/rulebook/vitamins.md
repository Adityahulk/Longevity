# Rulebook — Vitamins & Iron

## R-VIT-D-01 — Vitamin D insufficient
- **trigger**: `vitamin_d_25oh 20–29 ng/mL`
- **tier**: 2
- **N**: fatty fish, egg yolks; AM sunlight 15 min/day on bare skin (Indian latitudes, before 11am or after 4pm to minimize UVB skin damage).
- **S**: 60,000 IU/week × 8 weeks then 2,000 IU/day maintenance, with Vitamin K2 (MK-7) 100mcg/day. Retest at week 12.
- Critically common — > 70% of urban Indians are deficient.
- **evidence_tier**: E1

## R-VIT-D-02 — Vitamin D severely deficient — **TIER 1**
- **trigger**: `vitamin_d_25oh < 20 ng/mL`
- **tier**: 1
- Physician-supervised loading dose protocol recommended. Typical: 60,000 IU/week × 8–12 weeks, monitor Ca + 25(OH)D at week 12.
- Co-supplement K2 (MK-7) 100mcg/day to direct calcium to bone/teeth not soft tissue.
- Magnesium glycinate 300–400mg/day supports Vit D metabolism.
- Atopic / autoimmune / fatigue / low mood / poor sleep / relative-low T can all stem from severe deficiency — address the root.
- **evidence_tier**: E1

## R-VIT-B12-01 — Low B12
- **trigger**: `vitamin_b12 < 300 pg/mL`
- **tier**: 2 if 200–299; 1 if `< 200` — **TIER 1**
- **N**: eggs (vegetarian) · dairy · sea fish (non-veg) · fortified cereals.
- **S**: methylcobalamin 1000mcg/day sublingual for 12 weeks. Vegetarians/vegans: maintenance 500mcg/day after.
- **retest_signal**: B12 ↑ 200–400 pg/mL in 12 weeks. If no rise, check intrinsic factor antibodies (pernicious anemia).
- **evidence_tier**: E1

## R-VIT-B9-01 — Low folate
- **trigger**: `folate < 4 ng/mL`
- **tier**: 2
- **N**: leafy greens, lentils, citrus, fortified cereals, beets.
- **S**: methylfolate 400mcg/day (preferred over folic acid, especially if MTHFR polymorphism present).
- **evidence_tier**: E1

## R-VIT-04 — Ferritin deficiency
- **trigger**: `ferritin < 30 ng/mL` (M) OR `< 15 ng/mL` (F)
- **tier**: 2
- **root_cause_hypothesis**: low iron intake (vegetarian), heavy menses (F), GI loss.
- **N**: cooked spinach + lemon (Vit C boosts non-heme absorption) · dates · jaggery · whole pulses with citrus · iron pan cooking · don't drink tea/coffee within 1h of iron-rich meals.
- **S**: iron bisglycinate 25–50mg every other day with Vit C 500mg (every-other-day dosing absorbs better than daily). For females: rule out menorrhagia with gynecologist.
- **retest_signal**: ferritin ↑ 30–50 ng/mL in 90 days.
- **evidence_tier**: E1

## R-VIT-05 — Iron overload signal
- **trigger**: `ferritin > 300` (M) OR `> 200` (F) AND `transferrin_saturation > 45%`
- **tier**: 2
- Rule out hemochromatosis (HFE testing); also rule out inflammation as cause of high ferritin (inflammation raises ferritin independent of iron stores).
- Refer to physician. No iron-rich supplements until cleared.
- **evidence_tier**: E1

## R-VIT-MG-01 — Low magnesium
- **trigger**: `magnesium < 1.8 mg/dL`
- **tier**: 2
- Note: serum Mg is a poor marker — RBC Mg or symptomatic threshold often more useful. Symptoms: leg cramps, eyelid twitches, poor sleep, restless legs, palpitations.
- **N**: leafy greens, nuts, seeds, whole grains, dark chocolate.
- **S**: magnesium glycinate 300–400mg/day at bedtime (also helps sleep). Avoid magnesium oxide (poorly absorbed, causes loose stool).
- **evidence_tier**: E1
