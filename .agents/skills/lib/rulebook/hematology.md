# Rulebook — Hematology

## R-HEM-01 — Borderline-high RDW — NEW
- **trigger**: `rdw_cv 13.5–14.5%` (top of normal — Tier 3) or `> 14.5%` (Tier 2)
- **tier**: 3 if 13.5–14.5; 2 if >14.5
- **root_cause_hypothesis**: red-cell size heterogeneity is one of the strongest aging biomarkers in NHANES cohort data — driven by mixed nutrient deficiency (iron + B12 + folate), early oxidative stress, occult inflammation, or marrow stress.
- **N**: iron-rich foods (cooked spinach + lemon, dates, jaggery, whole pulses + citrus) · B12-rich foods (eggs, dairy, sea fish for non-veg) · leafy greens for folate.
- **S**: if ferritin <50 or B12 <500 or folate <8, address the deficiency directly (see R-VIT-03, R-VIT-04). If all nutrient markers are adequate, monitor — RDW often normalizes when oxidative stress lifestyle factors improve.
- **retest_signal**: RDW ↓ 0.5–1.0% in 90 days if nutrient-driven.
- **evidence_tier**: E2

## R-HEM-02 — Anemia
- **trigger**: `hemoglobin < 13` (M) OR `< 11.5` (F)
- **tier**: 2 if mild; 1 if `< 10` (any sex) — **TIER 1**
- **root_cause_hypothesis**: iron deficiency (most common), B12/folate deficiency, chronic disease, GI loss, thalassemia trait (common in Indians).
- Combine R-VIT-04 (ferritin/iron) interventions. If MCV <80, likely iron; if >100, likely B12/folate.
- If Hgb <10, refer to physician — full workup needed (rule out thalassemia/CKD/malignancy/GI loss).
- **evidence_tier**: E1

## R-HEM-03 — Polycythemia
- **trigger**: `hemoglobin > 18` (M) OR `> 16` (F) OR `hct > 54%` (M)
- **tier**: 2
- **root_cause_hypothesis**: smoking, dehydration, OSA (sleep apnea), high altitude, anabolic steroid use, polycythemia vera.
- Hydration audit · sleep study if OSA symptoms (snoring, daytime fatigue, morning headache) · physician referral if persistent.
- **evidence_tier**: E1

## R-HEM-04 — Monocytosis
- **trigger**: `monocytes_pct > 10%` OR `monocytes_abs > 0.9 × 10³/μL`
- **tier**: 3
- **root_cause_hypothesis**: chronic infection (incl. occult), autoimmune, post-viral, sometimes hematologic; in young healthy adults often benign post-viral residue.
- Correlate with HPI (recent illness, fever, weight loss). Recheck in 4–6 weeks if no symptoms.
- **evidence_tier**: E2

## R-HEM-05 — Borderline-high RBC mass + RDW + ferritin (Indian male phenotype)
- **trigger**: `hemoglobin 16–18` (M) AND `rdw_cv ≥ 14` AND `ferritin > 200`
- **tier**: 3
- Pattern suggests sub-clinical chronic inflammation + iron accumulation + relative dehydration. Often seen in lean active Indian men with poor hydration habits.
- Hydration (3L/day), audit alcohol, audit inflammation drivers.
- **evidence_tier**: E3
