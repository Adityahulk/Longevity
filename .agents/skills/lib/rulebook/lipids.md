# Rulebook — Lipids / Cardiovascular

## R-LIP-01 — High ApoB / LDL
- **trigger**: `apo_b > 100` OR `ldl > 130`
- **tier**: 2
- **root_cause_hypothesis**: saturated fat intake, refined-carb load, low fiber, genetic.
- **N**: shift sat fat → mono/poly (mustard, sesame, peanut, olive replace ghee/butter; ghee 1–2 tsp/day max) · 30g fiber, 10g soluble (oats, psyllium, beans, apples) · 30g almonds/walnuts/day · drop refined carbs.
- **T**: Zone 2 5×/wk 45min — directly lowers ApoB.
- **S**: psyllium 5–10g pre-meal. Omega-3 2g/day if TG also high.
- **M**: stress management — cortisol drives LDL.
- **retest_signal**: ApoB ↓ 15–25 mg/dL realistic in 90 days.
- **evidence_tier**: E1

## R-LIP-02 — ApoB ≥ 130 or LDL ≥ 190 — **TIER 1**
- **trigger**: `apo_b ≥ 130` OR `ldl ≥ 190`
- **tier**: 1
- Cardiology referral; familial hypercholesterolemia possible. Tessera protocol can run in parallel.
- **evidence_tier**: E1

## R-LIP-03 — Elevated triglycerides
- **trigger**: `triglycerides > 150`
- **tier**: 2
- **root_cause_hypothesis**: alcohol, refined carbs, fructose, insulin resistance.
- **N**: drop alcohol · drop refined carbs · drop fructose · fatty fish 2×/wk.
- **T**: Zone 2 + resistance (both lower TG).
- **S**: omega-3 2g EPA+DHA/day.
- **retest_signal**: TG ↓ 30–50% in 90 days.
- **evidence_tier**: E1

## R-LIP-04 — Low HDL
- **trigger**: `hdl < 40` (M) OR `< 50` (F)
- **tier**: 2
- **root_cause_hypothesis**: low cardio fitness, low monounsaturated fat intake, smoking, IR.
- **N**: olive oil, almonds, avocado, fatty fish.
- **T**: Zone 2 5×/wk — raises HDL more than any other intervention.
- **S**: niacin only under physician supervision (flushing, liver effects).
- **evidence_tier**: E1

## R-LIP-05 — Elevated Lp(a)
- **trigger**: `lp_a ≥ 50 mg/dL` (or ≥ 125 nmol/L)
- **tier**: 2 (with doctor consult)
- **root_cause_hypothesis**: 90% genetic, lifelong cardiovascular risk amplifier.
- Cannot be moved by lifestyle. Aggressive control of every *other* lipid marker becomes critical.
- Recommend cardiologist consult to discuss aggressive LDL targets (<70) and family screening.
- **evidence_tier**: E1

## R-LIP-06 — Elevated AIP (atherogenic dyslipidemia in Indians)
- **trigger**: `aip > 0.21` (i.e., log10(TG/HDL) > 0.21)
- **tier**: 2
- Common in South Asians at otherwise-normal LDL. Strong CV-event predictor in Indian cohorts.
- All R-MET-01 + R-LIP-03 interventions apply.
- **retest_signal**: AIP ↓ 0.1–0.2 in 90 days achievable.
- **evidence_tier**: E2
