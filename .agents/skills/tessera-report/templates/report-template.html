Protocol for re-test

Identical pre-test conditions are essential for a fair comparison:

  • Fasted 10–12 hours (water only)
  • Same lab partner, same time window (7–10 AM)
  • No alcohol for 72 hours prior
  • No heavy training in the 48 hours prior
  • Same supplements continued — note any changes

{{disclaimer_section_num}} · Important Notes

A few things to read carefully.

This report is not a medical diagnosis. It is an evidence-based interpretation of your blood panel and lifestyle inputs, designed to inform a 90-day metabolic-performance program. It does not replace consultation with a licensed physician.

Tier 1 findings must be discussed with a physician before any structured intervention. Where this report identifies such findings, please follow the medical-advisory callout in Section 02. If you experience symptoms (chest pain, breathlessness, severe fatigue, fainting, depression, suicidal thoughts), seek immediate medical care.

We do not make anti-aging or longevity-extension claims. The PhenoAge biological age estimate is a mortality-risk-derived snapshot, not a prediction. The aim is to move biomarkers in a healthy direction — moving biomarkers is the outcome we measure, and the only outcome we promise to measure.

Supplement recommendations are evidence-anchored but not personalized to your medication list at the clinical level. Please discuss every recommended supplement with your physician — especially if you take blood-thinners, diabetes medications, thyroid medications, or psychiatric medications. Do not change any prescribed dose based on this report.

Data & privacy. Your blood test results, intake responses, and this report are personal health data covered by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India). Tessera retains them per the privacy policy at tessera.health/privacy and will delete them within 15 days of a written request, subject to legal retention requirements. We do not share your identified health data with employers, insurers, or third-party advertisers.
